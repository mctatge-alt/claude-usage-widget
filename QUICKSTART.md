# Quick Start — macOS Apple Silicon

## 1. Download

From [Releases](https://github.com/mctatge-alt/claude-usage-widget/releases), download the latest:

`*-macOS-arm64.dmg`

## 2. Install

1. Open the DMG
2. Drag the app to **Applications**
3. Launch from Applications

If macOS blocks the app, **Right-click → Open** on the app (official signed build from Releases only).

## 3. Log in

1. Click **Login to Claude**
2. Sign in at claude.ai
3. The widget loads your usage automatically

## Daily use

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Show/hide widget | Click menu bar icon or **⌘⇧U** |
| Refresh | Toolbar refresh or **⌘R** |
| Settings | Toolbar gear or **⌘,** |
| Sign out | Menu bar right-click → Sign Out |

## What the bars mean

- **Session** (purple) — current window
- **Weekly** (blue) — rolling 7-day limit
- **Sonnet / Extra** — shown when applicable to your plan

## Privacy

Credentials and usage history stay on your Mac. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
